BibleIQ privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

BibleIQ (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a Bible quiz app published by [Your full name or business name]. You can contact us at [your email address].

This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • Your quiz progress and settings are stored on your own device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in an anonymous way, how the app is used so we can improve it. You can switch this off in Settings.
  • Subscription payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The app does not show ads, and we do not sell your information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work and remember where you left off:

  • your chosen level (Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced) and settings such as haptic feedback
  • your quiz progress, such as experience points, how many quizzes you finished, and which questions you have already seen
  • your remaining hints and whether you have completed today’s Daily Challenge
  • whether you have an active Premium subscription (as reported by Google Play)

This information stays on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Information collected through Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to measure how the app is used. This service collects information that does not directly identify you, including:

  • App activity: which screens you open; when you start, finish or leave a quiz; which game mode and difficulty you play; which question was shown and whether it was answered correctly; when you use or run out of hints; and when you open the Premium screen or start a purchase.
  • Your settings: your chosen level and whether you have Premium, so we can understand how different groups use the app.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and when the app was first opened and how long sessions last.
  • Approximate location: Google uses your IP address to work out a rough location such as your country or city. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • An app instance identifier: a random identifier created by the app on your device. It is not your name or email, and it resets if you uninstall and reinstall the app.

We do not use this data to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any information that could.

For more about how Google handles this data, see Google’s privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. When you subscribe:

  • Google processes your payment. We do not receive or store your card number or payment details.
  • Google tells the app whether your subscription is active so the app can unlock Premium features. We may also receive limited purchase information from Google, such as the subscription type and status.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app under Payments and subscriptions.

Google’s handling of your payment information is covered by Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise (GPS) location, photos, files, microphone or camera data. The app does not show advertising and does not use your device’s advertising ID.

How we use information

  • to run the app and keep your progress and Premium status working
  • to understand which features and questions people find useful, too easy or too hard
  • to find and fix problems and improve the app
  • to process and verify subscriptions through Google Play

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google, which provides Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Play (including billing), acting as a service provider for these purposes.
  • Authorities or others, where the law requires it or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics: open Settings in the app and switch off Share anonymous usage data. The app will stop sending analytics data.
  • Reset the app identifier: uninstalling and reinstalling the app creates a new random identifier.
  • Delete on-device data: clear the app’s storage in your phone settings or uninstall the app.
  • Cancel Premium: do this in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.
  • Device-level controls: you can also review privacy and permission settings in your Android system settings.

How long we keep information

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for the retention period set in our account (currently [2 months / 14 months]), after which it is deleted automatically. We may keep summarised statistics that do not identify any person for longer.

Children

BibleIQ is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Google applies its own safeguards to such transfers. By using the app you understand that your information may be processed in these locations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or under data protection laws such as Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012), you may have the right to ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because the app does not collect your name or email and the identifier is random, we may need you to give us the details needed to find the relevant data. Contact us at [your email address] and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, and Google applies industry-standard security to the services it provides. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or on the store listing. Please check this page occasionally.
